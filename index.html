Step 3 — What changes without the PILOT?

PILOT is on by default. Switch it off to model a different development choice: 100 unrestricted market-rate apartments instead of 80 market-rate and 20 set-aside apartments, but with the higher post-improvement property tax. The developer's chosen market rent and all financing assumptions stay the same. This is a comparison of alternatives, not a mechanism for ending an existing affordability agreement.

A PILOT reduces the tax burden but requires 20 income-restricted apartments in this model. Without the PILOT, those 20 apartments are modeled at market rent, but property taxes increase. Neither scenario guarantees that the developer can charge the assumed market rent: developers should assess actual market demand.
